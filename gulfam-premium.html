Our Story

From a small counter to a family name

Gulfam began in 1990 as a single counter selling fresh milk and a handful of mithai. Every sweet was stirred by hand over slow flame, and customers returned because they could taste the difference.

Today we make mithai, nimco, bakery, cakes and frozen snacks, but the promise is the same: pure ingredients, fresh batches, honest prices.

How we make it

Milk arrives from trusted farms each morning. It is boiled, reduced and set by hand in small batches by halwais with decades of experience. Nothing is made from powder and nothing is stored overnight.
